How We Work

Four things that don't change, engagement to engagement.

The same discipline applies whether it's one social graphic or a full campaign — that consistency is what agencies are actually paying for.

White-label, always

Everything you make ships under the client's name — no attribution, no watermark, no public credit line. Your craft is judged internally, by people who actually know what went into it, not by a portfolio comment count.

Benchmarked, not guessed

Every deliverable type has a real, documented time benchmark — a logo, a social graphic, a minute of video. You're not fighting an arbitrary deadline; you're working against a number that's actually been measured.

Remote-first, for real

Most production roles work from home as a deliberate part of how we're built, not an exception you have to ask for. What matters is the work landing on time and at standard — not where you were sitting when you made it.

Backed by BESBPO Group

Palette Canvas runs on the infrastructure and backing of a much larger, established Group — you're joining a division with real support behind it, not a solo operation figuring things out as it goes.

Who We're Looking For

Skill gets you the interview. These get you the offer.

Our Interview Scorecard weighs technical craft alongside every trait below — deliberately, not as an afterthought.

01

A genuine confidentiality mindset

You don't need your name on something to care about doing it well. Discretion isn't a policy you tolerate here — it's part of the craft.

02

Comfortable inside real process

Two revision rounds, a three-part QA check, a benchmarked hour count — you find that clarifying, not restrictive.

03

Self-directed, remote-capable

Nobody's watching a webcam to confirm you're working. You manage your own time against a deadline that's real.

04

Craft first, ego second

The work being genuinely good matters more to you than who gets to say they made it.
